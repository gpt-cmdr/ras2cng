## Masking rule (as RAS Mapper 7.0.1 stored maps behave)

**Depth surface.**
- slopingPretty renders the water surface with face values as well as cell values. Its two
  options are shallow reduction (reduce shallow to horizontal) and depth-weighted faces.
- Each pixel's WSE is a weighted combination of its cell's face and facepoint water surfaces;
  depth weighting rebalances those weights.
- With shallow reduction, a cell whose faces are all unconnected or shallow and downhill is
  painted flat.

**Wet test.**
- A cell is skipped when its WSE is not more than 0.001 above the cell minimum elevation.
- Depth is WSE minus terrain, kept where > 0.

**Velocity.**
- Velocity values come from the face velocities and do not depend on the render mode.
- A pixel is written only where the active mode's depth is positive, i.e. the depth rendered
  with the *same* slopingPretty options.

**Derived products.**
- D x V, D x V^2 and Froude use that same per-time depth and velocity.
- Their Max is the per-output-time pixelwise maximum, with the valid area being the union over times.

**Consequence.**
- Shallow reduction and depth weighting change the **mask** (and depth), never the velocity values.
- Edge handling is that of the depth surface: there is no separate velocity edge rule.
- The engine already implements this: it clips velocity with the rendered depth of the request's options.
