## Verification

Scripts in `G:\RustDev\work\T19\scripts`: `verify.py`, `verify_mesh.py`, `verify_results.py`; output in `verify.txt`.

- **Mesh tilesets:** `geometry-detail.pmtiles` is byte-identical to T15's on UPGU3, UPGU4, Spring and Davis.
- **Non-mesh layers, against T15's tiles:** per layer and zoom, no distinct property tuple was dropped or added, and no zoom was lost. Tile counts are unchanged, except Spring `mesh-areas` and `model-extents` at z17 (29,805 against 29,806), from the 1 ft filter. Tiles carry no feature IDs, so properties are the identity available; a layer with a repeated tuple could hide a dropped duplicate.
- **Mesh cells against the archive:** complete at every zoom on UPGU3, UPGU4 and Spring.
- **Reference lines:** 60 of 60 values on the 15 UPGU3 lines match the archive after decoding the tiles.
- **Manifest:** `vectorSimplification.perZoom` lists the layers; `mannings-n-regions` records 1,062,037 -> 122,122 (UPGU3), 533,600 -> 79,364 (UPGU4).

**"No feature dropped per zoom" is not fully true, and was not before this task.** freestiler omits a line whose two ends snap to the same tile pixel:
- UPGU3 `mesh_faces`: 4 of 637,473 absent at z13, none from z14.
- UPGU4 `mesh_faces`: 8 absent at z14, 5 at z15, 2 at z16 and 2 at z17 (faces of 0.10 ft, shorter than one tile pixel).
- Spring: none.
- Sparse line layers thin out strongly at low zoom: UPGU3 breaklines 156 of 1,676 at z0.

The mesh tiles are byte-identical to the earlier output, and breaklines and every other non-mesh layer match T15's property sets, so this is not from this change. CLAUDE.md claimed "never drop features" without this exception; it and the release notes now state it. Polygons are complete. Fixing it belongs in the freestiler fork (for example a minimum one-pixel line, as polygons get a one-pixel dot) and is **not done**.

The results-detail layers were checked at z13 and z17 only: `results-detail.pmtiles` cell variables are complete, and face variables show the same 4 absent at z13. Rendering in a browser was not checked.

## Decisions to look at

- **0.5 px, as asked.** Stated in the docs and release notes with the reason.
- **Per-zoom needs Arrow input.** The GeoJSON input and `ras2cng pmtiles` do not pass it. I left them, because Arrow is the default for any freestiler that has the option.
- **`--no-simplify` turns off both filters;** `--simplify-tolerance 0` turns off only the first.
- **Column naming:** the pivot gives snake_case names, unlike the archive's `Variable`/`Statistic`. Viewers that read reference results see new property names, but none could read them before, since this path failed.
