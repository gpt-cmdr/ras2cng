# T14: terrain modifications on the native effective terrain (2026-10-30)

**Status: partial.**
- The engine and ras-commander now rebuild RAS Mapper's terrain **with** modifications for three forms:
  - ground lines (high ground and channels, SetIfHigher/SetIfLower);
  - constant polygons;
  - ellipses.
- On the real corpora these modifications match exactly, or within an isolated 1/32 ft at exact ties, on 4 of the 5 terrains staged. On the fifth (Kingswood TerrainAlt2) 433 px differ.
- Every other modification form is refused, so the terrain goes to the HEC-RAS export.
- **No real modified terrain is yet pixel-identical as a whole.** Every runnable one fails the bar for one of two reasons:
  - it carries a refused form; or
  - the **base** effective terrain (T07's stitch-TIN / mosaic rule) differs from RAS Mapper on these terrains, in stitch-TIN pixels or, on two Kingswood terrains, in mosaic pixels.
- That base gap is new evidence against T07's qualification. It is not a modification issue.

## 2. Rules derived (pixel fits against exports; no vendor code)

Every reference is `RasTerrain.export_rasmapper_terrain(downsample_factor=1, rasterize_modifications=True)`, HEC-RAS 7.0.1, run on project copies. Per-layer references were made two ways:
- an HDF copy reduced to one layer;
- fast `extent=` exports, which take 2-45 s against 160-280 s for a full export.

General findings:
- **The rasmap `Checked` state is ignored by the export.** Every HDF layer is applied. The Prop_Topo export with HighGround/Channels unchecked equals the all-checked export, apart from stitch-TIN noise.
- **Layer order is descending Priority value.** Priority 0 is applied last and wins. Proof: on synthetic W4, a SetIfLower channel at Priority 0 cuts through a SetValue 700 ft pad at Priority 1, on all 1,894 overlap pixels. The ascending order is also 2.7x worse on TerrainAlt2.
- **Nodata terrain inside a modification takes the modification value.** Examples:
  - London-Allen lies entirely over nodata, and all 20,843 px match;
  - Kingswood culvert_burn has 0.0001 slopes and writes values of 447,791 ft.
- **Rounding:** binary32, then 1/32 with ties to even, as for the terrain itself.
- **Extent exports are not base-consistent with full exports.** Nodata flips appear near the stitch TIN. Extent references were therefore compared only with extent base exports.

Per type:

| Type | Rule | Qualification | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground line, SetIfHigher / SetIfLower (levee and channel) | Triangulated template. The centerline is split at every profile station (stations within 1e-3 ft of a vertex are not split); z is linear by station, clamped beyond the profile. The crest edge sits at Top Width/2, level with the centerline, and the toe at **Max Reach/2** (total width), `(MaxReach/2 - TopWidth/2)/slope` below (levee) or above (channel). Slopes are **H:V**, Left/Right relative to digitizing direction. Convex joints are **beveled** (a fan around the vertex), concave joints **mitered**, ends **square**. Each quad is split on its **Delaunay** diagonal. A pixel centre takes the linear value of its triangle; overlaps inside one feature take the max (levee) or min (channel). | Prop: 4,836 modified px, 0 diff.<br>Kingswood Alt1/Alt3/existing_1m: 0 diff, except 3 px at 1/32 on Alt1.<br>NGOM L&A, London-Allen, Monticello 2: 0 diff; Levee Centerlines 3 px at 1/32.<br>**Alt2: 433 px** (below) | supported |
| Ground line, SetValue / Add | Not derived. Cecil St Wall (Add) is close (162 px at 1/32 on 401). | n/a | **refused** |
| Ground lines sharing a `System Name` | One profile is shared across the system. At a junction the next feature's start elevation wins: this fixed half of Hoeys Canal (20,094 to 10,365 px). The geometry with Transition Percent = 1 is not derived. | n/a | **refused** |
| Ground line with Max Reach <= Top Width | The crest is clipped by the reach in a way not derived. Monticello "Trap": up to 8 ft; Upperline: 22 px of 4,045. | n/a | **refused** |
| Polygon, constant (Generate Boundary Elevations = 0) | Pixel centre inside (even-odd), constant `Elevation Value`; a stored profile is **ignored**. | Kingswood Culvert Enforce BKG 108 px, Polygons 722 px; NGOM 17th St Gates 10,759 px, Sump 17,828 px, Sumps 6,626 px: all 0 diff | supported (SetValue/Hi/Lo) |
| Polygon, boundary elevations (gen = 1) / control points / shape Z | Not derived (NGOM Fix, ELC, East Galveston Bay). | n/a | **refused** |
| Ellipse (Circle), rotation 0 | Pixel centre strictly inside `(dx/a)^2 + (dy/b)^2 < 1`; the stored values are semi-axes. | Prop 86 px, 0 diff | supported; rotated ellipses refused |

How the ground-line rule was found:
- T07's nearest-segment trapezoid had the slopes as V:H; the slope is in fact H:V.
- Flat caps matched exactly on Prop's channels at once.
- Bevel joints fixed Prop's high-ground joints (82 to 2 px).
- The triangulated template with Delaunay quads fixed Kingswood channel z-distortion near joints (20 to 0 px) and the remaining Prop ties.
- An unconstrained global Delaunay was tested and is far worse (53,029 px on W3).
- Applying modifications per source before the mosaic was also tested, and is worse.

**Open discrepancies (measured, not explained):**
1. **Kingswood TerrainAlt2: 433 px off** (96 above 1/32, max 0.84 ft) of 9,475 modified px.
   - The pixels lie around levee HighGround#1 (5 vertices, 19-25 ft first segments against a 50 ft half reach) where channel Channels#3 crosses it.
   - RAS Mapper's levee surface there is 0.05-0.1 ft lower than the template.
   - Neither profile scaling nor extrapolation fixes it. Clamping is best.
2. **Isolated 1/32 ft roundings at exact ties.**
   - Seen at 3 px on TerrainAlt1, 3 on Levee Centerlines, and 2 on Prop before the triangulated model.
   - Our raw value is within 0.002/32 ft of a tie, and RAS Mapper rounds the other way.
   - They are **stable** across repeat exports (TerrainAlt1 repeat: 1 px differs, and it is elsewhere), so they are a numeric difference, not RAS Mapper noise.
   - Float32 variants of the TIN evaluation were tried and did not fix them.
3. **Writer-authored 22 km channel on synthetic T2 (20 ft): 1,502 px off.** The worst error is about 1 ft at the first joint; elsewhere it is 1/32-0.2 ft along the toe edges. Transition Percent is not the cause (setting it to NaN changed nothing).

## 3. ras-commander writer bugs: confirmed and fixed (in #790)

Each bug was compared with the RAS-Mapper-authored layouts, and each fix was verified with HEC-RAS 7.0.1 exports.
- **`add_channel_modification` had no effect.** The writer set `Type=Channel`. RAS Mapper writes every ground line as `Type=Levee` with `Subtype=Levee|Channel`, and ignores other Types. Now `Type=Levee`; the channel is applied (W3: 11,202 px changed).
- **`add_modification_polygon(elevation_method="provided")` set the polygon to 0 ft.**
  - The writer wrote `Elevation Value=0` with `Generate Boundary Elevations=0`. RAS Mapper then uses that constant and ignores the stored profile (NGOM 17th St Gates proves it).
  - A level provided/shape-Z boundary is now stored as the constant. W1 (700 ft pond): RAS Mapper applies it, and the engine matches 46,816 px exactly.
  - Varying boundaries log a warning, because their RAS-Mapper-honoured layout is unverified.
- **Every written layer got `Priority=0`.** Order is undefined, and the new staging refuses shared priorities. Layers now get unique increasing priorities.
- **The writer's default `Transition Percent=0.5`.** RAS Mapper's own layers store NaN. No effect was observed; left as is.

Writer-authored modifications are now qualified for level polygons. Writer channels/levees on long segments carry open discrepancy 3.

## 6. Viability verdict

- **Modifications themselves are largely solved for the forms RAS Mapper users draw most.** Ground-line levees and channels with SetIfHigher/SetIfLower, plus constant polygons and ellipses, are reproduced exactly on 4 of 5 accepted real terrains (5,000-12,000 modified px each). The exceptions are isolated 1/32 ft ties and one levee/channel pair on TerrainAlt2.
- **On the real corpora, 0 of 11 runnable modified terrains are reproduced exactly as a whole today:**
  - 6 are refused for forms not yet derived: Add, System joins, boundary-elevation polygons, control points, Max Reach <= Top Width, a feature without a profile, shared priorities. These go to the HEC-RAS export.
  - The 5 accepted ones (Prop_Topo, Kingswood TerrainAlt1-3, existing_1m) all still differ from the export in the **base** effective terrain (stitch-TIN or mosaic pixels). TerrainAlt2 also differs in modification pixels.
- What is still needed for "viable", in order of payoff:
  1. Fix the T07 base rule on these terrains: Kingswood's stitch TIN (deterministic in RAS Mapper, so derivable) and the near-equal-cell-size mosaic. Without this, even unmodified versions of these terrains are not exact.
  2. Control points (East Galveston Bay, ELC) and boundary-elevation polygons (NGOM Fix): the largest refusal classes by layer count.
  3. System joins, ground-line SetValue/Add, and Max Reach <= Top Width (NGOM, Cahaba/Homewood).
  4. The TerrainAlt2 short-segment joint case and the near-tie numerics.
- Until then the engine's modification support is safe: anything unproven is refused before the engine runs. But it would carry real terrains only where a small base/TIN tolerance is acceptable. It should **not** replace the HEC-RAS export by default for modified terrains yet. That is the user's decision.
