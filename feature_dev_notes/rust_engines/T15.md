## 2. Rust (terrio-clb#10)

**New module `crates/terrio/src/ops/simplify_dp.rs`:**
- It runs GEOS 3.14's `DouglasPeuckerSimplifier` directly on WKB, with no geometry objects.
- It covers Polygon, MultiPolygon, LineString, MultiLineString and GeometryCollection; points pass through.
- Distances use GEOS's `pointToSegment` formulas operation for operation, and ties go to the first vertex.
- **Ring start point:** a ring's start point is dropped as GEOS's `simplifyRingEndpoint` does. This requires at least 5 points and a distance within the tolerance (`<=`), and the ring is then closed on its second point. Closed linestrings keep their endpoints.
- **Collapsed rings** (fewer than 4 points): the default `collapse="keep"` keeps the input vertices, so no polygon or hole disappears. `"drop"` does as GEOS does: the hole is dropped, or the polygon becomes empty or leaves its multipolygon. Both are counted in the stats.
- **No repair step.** GEOS runs `buffer(0)` on an invalid polygonal result; terrio does not, and this is documented.
- Byte order, ISO/EWKB type codes and SRID, Z and M are all kept.
- A feature with a non-finite X or Y passes through and is counted.
- Malformed WKB, trailing bytes and curve types are refused with the feature's index. Counts are checked before anything is allocated.

**Parallelism:**
- Features run in parallel.
- Inside one ring, sections over 32k vertices search for their farthest vertex in parallel pieces. The pieces are combined in order, so ties are unchanged.
- Such sections then split into two parallel sections, with recursion into the smaller side. The result does not depend on the split; a test checks it against a recursive reference port.
- Parallel DP inside one ring took UPGU3 from 103 ms to 50 ms.

**API surfaces:**
- **Rust:** `simplify_wkb`, `simplify_wkb_many` and `simplify_douglas_peucker`.
- **Geometry op:** `GeometryOp::SimplifyDouglasPeucker`.
- **CLI:** `terrio simplify --method douglas-peucker --collapse keep|drop`. The default method stays preserve-topology.
- **Python `terrio.simplify_wkb(geometries, tolerance, *, method, collapse, stats)`:**
  - Input: Arrow `binary`, `large_binary` or `binary_view` arrays, or a ChunkedArray, or a sequence of bytes.
  - Output: a `pyarrow.Array` of the input type, a new `terrio.ArrowArray` when pyarrow is absent, or a list.
- **Python and JS:** `simplify(..., method=)`.

**Topology-preserving mode:**
- It already existed in terrio (a GEOS TPS port) and is exposed as `method="preserve-topology"`.
- It is not worth more for ras2cng: on these layers it gives the same counts (+1 vertex in 3.7 M) at 10-15x the time.

**No `write_geoparquet_layers` option.** ras2cng simplifies in a separate pass before tiling or writing, where the layer kind and the CRS units are known.

### Equivalence with GEOS (3.14.1, shapely 2.2)

**Six geometry archives, every layer but mesh faces (mesh cells included):**
- The archives are UPGU3, UPGU4, Spring, Spring_BLE, Bald Eagle g04 and Davis g02.
- 2,224,927 features are **vertex-for-vertex identical** at 0.1, 0.5, 1, 3.28 and 10 ft (with `collapse="drop"`).
- The only exception: at 10 ft, 2 features where GEOS's own result was invalid (GEOS repairs those) are excluded.

**Also identical:** random polygons and lines with holes at 4 tolerances, the ring-endpoint, orientation and collapse edge cases, and TPS against GEOS (Python tests).

### Speed and memory against GEOS

- Method: a fresh process per engine, median of 5 calls, at 0.1 ft.
- Host: about 50% background CPU, 8 cores.
- "GEOS WKB" means `from_wkb` + `simplify` + `to_wkb`, which does the same I/O as terrio.

| Case | Vertices in -> out | GEOS DP | GEOS DP WKB | **terrio DP** | GEOS TPS | terrio TPS |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| UPGU3 land cover | 1,062,037 -> 407,116 | 181 ms | 238 ms | **50 ms (3.6x / 4.8x)** | 2,721 ms | 1,255 ms |
| UPGU4 land cover | 533,600 -> 247,605 | 96 ms | 125 ms | **27 ms (3.6x / 4.7x)** | 1,333 ms | 678 ms |
| Every non-mesh layer of 5 archives (15,801 features) | 3,683,608 -> 2,104,113 | 501 ms | 765 ms | **115 ms (4.4x / 6.7x)** | 6,899 ms | 1,575 ms |
| UPGU4 mesh cells, 1,063,548 polygons (throughput only) | 6,151,331 -> 5,962,163 | 3,871 ms | 6,235 ms | **411 ms (9.4x / 15x)** | 5,507 ms | 907 ms |

**Peak working set** (process, including the loaded data):
- UPGU3: terrio 633 MiB against GEOS 597/625 MiB.
- Mesh cells: terrio 2,175 MiB against GEOS 2,434/2,589 MiB.
- Memory is not a differentiator: the simplification itself is small next to the data.

**Is it blazing fast?** It is 3.6-15x faster than GEOS. In absolute terms GEOS was already fast here (0.1-0.2 s per land-cover polygon), so on these models the filter saves milliseconds. **The time that matters is the tiler's, and that depends on the vertex count, not on who simplifies.**

## 3. ras2cng (gpt-cmdr/ras2cng#37)

**Defaults:**
- **Web-map tiles** (`maplibre`, `maplibre-stored-vector`): on by default at **0.1 ft**.
  - The tolerance is converted to the CRS units: 0.1 in a foot CRS (US survey or international), 0.03048 m in a metre CRS.
  - Geographic CRSs and layers without a CRS are skipped with a warning.
  - The filter runs in the model CRS before reprojection.
- **Archive:** off by default; opt in with `ras2cng archive --simplify-tolerance FT`. The environment variable never applies to archives. Only Python-path layers are simplified, never the native mesh layers.

**Scope:**
- Simplified: the polygons of every layer except **`mesh_cells`/`mesh_faces`** (never touched). In practice that is land cover, mesh-area perimeters, refinement regions, infiltration regions, storage areas, terrain modification and source polygons, model extents and Stored Map polygons.
- **Lines are not included.** Breaklines would lose 30-37%, but lines are not a tiling bottleneck: Spring's whole geometry tiling, with 1.1 M breakline vertices, takes 70 s. Results also join to lines by identity. This could be an opt-in later.

**Flags and env vars:**
- `--simplify-tolerance FT` and `--no-simplify`.
- `RAS2CNG_SIMPLIFY_TOLERANCE` (feet; `0` or `off` turns it off).
- `RAS2CNG_SIMPLIFY_ENGINE=auto|terrio|shapely`.

**Engines:**
- `auto` uses terrio's `simplify_wkb` when present (capability-detected), otherwise shapely.
- The shapely fallback runs GEOS DP ring by ring, keeping collapsed rings, so its vertices are identical to terrio's (tested).

**Records:**
- Viewer manifest: `vectorSimplification`, plus a per-layer `simplification` with the tolerance in ft and in CRS units, the units, engine, features, vertices in and out, rings kept and seconds.
- Archive manifest: `manifest.geometry[].simplification` with a per-layer list.

**Docs:** CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, `docs/user-guide/maplibre.md`, `project-archive.md`, and RELEASE_NOTES (Unreleased).

**MVT resolution:** at the default maximum zoom 17, one MVT coordinate unit is about 0.12-0.13 ft at Texas latitudes. A 0.1 ft filter is therefore below what the tiles can show.

**UPGU3: 2.6x faster, still dominated by the land cover.**
- 0.1 ft leaves 407 k vertices, and freestiler's time on that one polygon is linear in its vertex count. That is consistent with 1,534 s * 0.383 ≈ 590 s.
- The simplification itself took 0.17 s (terrio).
- `geometry.pmtiles` shrank from 13.95 MB to 11.29 MB.
- `geometry-detail.pmtiles` (the mesh) is byte-identical to B2's.
